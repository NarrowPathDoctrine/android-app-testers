YOUR INFORMATION

Tester privacy notice

This notice explains how Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries intends to use details submitted through its Android app tester registration form.

Draft for review: Registration is not yet accepting applications. A contact email and final form provider configuration will be added before registration opens.

Who collects the information?

Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries is responsible for the information submitted for this voluntary testing programme. For privacy enquiries or requests to be removed, contact [Contact email to be added before launch].

What we collect

Your name, Google Play email address, preferred app categories, your device type/manufacturer/model and Android version, testing experience, willingness to report issues, and any optional comments you choose to provide.

Why we use it

We use your information to review requests to volunteer, find suitable devices and testers, contact you about appropriate Android app tests, help manage Google Play tester invitations, and respond to testing feedback. Participation is optional and registering does not guarantee an invitation.

How the form is handled

Once enabled, the form is submitted to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, which processes the submission and forwards a notification to our designated email inbox. We do not publish tester applications on GitHub. Form and email service providers may process information in other countries; their relevant privacy terms and safeguards apply.

Legal basis and retention

For UK privacy law purposes, the registration contact permission is based on your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us. We keep applications only while they are needed for the tester programme and remove them when no longer required or when you request removal, subject to any necessary legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can decline any invitation or ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on applicable law, you can also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information and raise concerns with the relevant data protection authority. Please contact [Contact email to be added before launch] to make a request.
