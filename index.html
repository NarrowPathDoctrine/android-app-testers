GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful
answers.

Everything you need to know before volunteering.

Do I need previous testing experience?+

No. People who use Android apps every day can spot issues that developers may miss. All experience levels are welcome.

Why do you need my Google Play email?+

For Google Play testing tracks, we may need the Google Account associated with your device to include you on a tester list. Your application alone doesn't add you to any testing track.

Will I be contacted about every app?+

Not necessarily. We'll consider your app-type preference and devices when contacting people about relevant tests.

Can I stop testing later?+

Yes. Testing is voluntary. You can decline invitations, leave a Google Play test, or ask to be removed from the invitation list.
