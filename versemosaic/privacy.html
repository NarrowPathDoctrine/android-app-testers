Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the VerseMosaic Android app handles Scripture study information, locally stored content, reminders, and related data.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who we are

VerseMosaic is a King James Bible (KJV) Scripture study and discovery app published by Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries. Questions about privacy can be sent to narrowpathdoctrine@gmail.com.

2. Information used by the app

VerseMosaic does not require a user account or ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location to read and study Scripture.

When you use its features, VerseMosaic can process and store information such as:

  • Scripture verses you have saved or bookmarked;
  • Notes you write while studying;
  • Reading and exploration progress, such as visited verses and completed books;
  • Preferences and other app settings; and
  • Daily Discovery reminder preferences and notification settings, if you enable reminders.

These features are intended for personal use within the app. VerseMosaic does not provide a developer-operated account or cloud-sync service for this study data, and Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not receive a copy of your notes, saved verses, or reading activity through such a service.

3. Local storage and Bible study content

VerseMosaic stores your personal study data on your Android device to make saved Scripture, notes, progress, and preferences available when you return. Scripture text, commentary, cross-references, and other built-in study material are used to support the app's features.

Other people who can access your unlocked device or its backups may be able to access locally stored information. Device security, backup settings, and permissions are managed through Android and your device provider.

4. Daily Discovery reminders and notifications

If you choose to enable a Daily Discovery reminder, VerseMosaic uses Android's notification functionality to remind you of the day's Scripture content. You can adjust reminder settings in the app and control or revoke notification permission in Android settings.

Reminder settings are part of your device-local app preferences. The reminder feature does not require you to register an account with Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries.

5. Sharing, advertising, and external services

Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not sell your personal Scripture study information, notes, or bookmarks to advertisers or data brokers. VerseMosaic is not designed to share your locally saved study content with a developer-operated database.

If you deliberately copy or share a verse or other content using Android or another app, that transfer is under your control; the receiving application may handle that information according to its own privacy policy.

If you follow a link from VerseMosaic to an external site or service, that destination may receive ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, and may use cookies or other technologies under its own privacy practices. Google Play and Android may also process installation, update, security, or diagnostic information as part of their independent platform services. This policy does not govern those services.

6. Data security

VerseMosaic relies on Android's app storage and operating-system security controls for locally stored study data. You can help protect that data by keeping your device updated and using a secure screen lock. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not maintain a VerseMosaic user-account database containing your personal notes or saved verses.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally saved study information generally remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear VerseMosaic's app data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing the app's data removes the information held in the app's local storage. VerseMosaic does not require an account-deletion request because it does not create a VerseMosaic account.

Your Android device or Google account may independently maintain backups, depending on your backup settings and the app's backup eligibility. Copies retained by a device or cloud backup provider are governed by that provider's controls and retention practices. Removing local app data may not immediately delete a separate device backup.

For questions or privacy requests relating to VerseMosaic, email narrowpathdoctrine@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

VerseMosaic is a Scripture study application for a general audience. Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a VerseMosaic user account or developer-operated study-data service.

9. Privacy of this website

This policy is hosted as a static webpage using GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to its hosting service. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details. This page does not use an advertising or analytics script.

10. Policy changes and contact

If VerseMosaic's functions or data handling change, this policy may be revised. The most recent version and its update date will appear on this page.
